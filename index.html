For creators

Share the picture. Not what's hidden in the file.

Untraceable makes a clean copy of a photo or video before you post or send it. Location, phone model, serial numbers, capture dates, names and extra tracks are removed on your iPhone. The picture and sound stay the same.

{{ store }} How it works

Runs only on your iPhone. No account. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is kept after you finish.

What gets removed

A photo carries more than the picture.

Phones and editing apps write details into every file. Untraceable rebuilds the file from the picture and sound alone, so those details are not carried over.

Location and capture time

GPS coordinates, altitude, the date and time a photo was taken, and location tracks inside videos.

Phone, lens and serial numbers

Make and model, lens details, camera serial numbers and the software version that saved the file.

Names and linking IDs

Owner and author names, captions, editing data, and the IDs that tie a file to its Live Photo, burst or editing project.

Hidden extras

Embedded previews, depth data, content credentials and maker notes that most viewers never show.

Extra video tracks

A clean video keeps one picture track and one sound track. Subtitle, location and other tracks are dropped; track names and container dates are blanked.

File name and dates

Camera numbering like IMG_4821 becomes a neutral name like photo-7f3a9c.jpg, and the file's own dates are reset.

How it works

Pick, clean, share. About a minute.

Every clean copy is opened again and checked before you can share it. A copy that fails the check is deleted, never handed to you.

1Choose

Pick photos and videos with the system picker or from Files. The app never asks for access to your library.

2Review

Everything hidden is always removed. Add a per-buyer mark or device unlinking if you want them.

3Clean and verify

Each item is rebuilt on your iPhone, then re-opened and checked against what a clean file may contain.

4Share or save

See exactly what was found and removed, then share the copy or save it to Photos. Originals are deleted from the app.

Untraceable Pro

Know who leaked it.

Cleaning one item at a time is free, with every removal included. Pro adds tools for creators who send custom work to individual buyers.

  • Per-buyer mark. A random code is tiled across the picture for one recipient. If a copy turns up somewhere it shouldn't, type the code to see who it was made for. The recipient list never leaves your iPhone.
  • Device unlinking. Normalises size and softens sensor noise, which makes it harder to link two files to the same phone. It reduces that link; it can't remove it.
  • Batch cleaning. Clean a whole set in one go, with progress for each item.

Honest limits

What a clean copy can't hide

  • It still looks like it was encoded on an iPhone, like millions of others.
  • Reverse-image search can still match the picture itself.
  • The link to your phone is reduced, not removed.
  • Re-encoding loses a tiny bit of quality.
  • A determined person can crop or cover a visible mark.
You protect the rest

Traces that live outside the file

  • The account you post from: reused usernames and synced contacts link profiles.
  • Your IP address: sites see the network you upload from.
  • Your AirDrop name: receivers see your device name.
  • Posting one file twice: the same picture on two accounts can be matched.

On your iPhone only

Cleaning, checking and leak lookup all run on the device. The app has no server and no account.

Nothing kept

Originals are deleted once their clean copy passes its check, and clean copies are deleted when you tap Done.
