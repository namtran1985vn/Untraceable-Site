## 1. What the app does

The app makes a clean copy of a photo or video you choose. It removes the hidden data listed on the [home page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}#removed), such as location, device details, dates, names and extra tracks. It does not change what the picture shows or what the audio says, except for the optional per-buyer mark and device unlinking that you turn on yourself. All processing happens on your iPhone.

## 2. What the app cannot do

No tool can make a file impossible to trace. A clean copy still looks like it was encoded on an iPhone; reverse-image search can still match the picture; device unlinking reduces the link to your phone but does not remove it; and a visible mark can be cropped or covered. The app cannot protect against traces outside the file, such as your account, your IP address, your AirDrop name, or posting the same file in two places. You are responsible for how and where you share your copies.
